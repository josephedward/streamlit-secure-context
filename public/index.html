SecureAI Notes

How it works

  • This demo runs entirely in your browser (no server).
  • We analyze your note with a simple keyword extractor (demo fallback) inside a sandboxed iframe.
  • A “toxicity score” is computed as a rough tags-to-words ratio.
  • All AI logic lives in an isolated iframe under a secure context (HTTPS/localhost) to prevent untrusted code from touching your page directly.
  • Logs and results show up here for transparency—and you can inspect performance or errors right on the page.
